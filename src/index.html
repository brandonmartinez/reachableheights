A preserved copy of a web site that stopped being updated in 2018. Nothing here is live — the band is not playing, no address is monitored, and nothing is for sale. How this was recovered

Ludington, Michigan · 2016–2020

“A praise and worship band writing songs to serve our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.”

Three friends off a church worship team who kept writing after the Sunday set was over. They released five recordings between May and October of 2016, posted about each one, and then kept working for four more years without releasing anything else.

Three people

The band

Reachable Heights came out of the worship team at Radiant Coast in Ludington. Same three people, same Sunday mornings — but a catalog of their own instead of somebody else’s set list. The band never listed its members anywhere public; the site and the SoundCloud account only ever carried the name.

Jessica Hein

Lead vocals. The strongest voice in the room, and the one pushing for original material early — in November 2016 she was already saying they should “try to write a real worship song.”

Andrew Lucero

Bass and guitar. He had been playing bass alongside Brandon at Radiant since 2016 and called it “a bucket-list item, complete.”

Brandon Martinez

Guitar, bass, drums, keys. Recording, production, and mixing. The band lived in his studio and on his domain — which is why this page exists at all.

2016 – 2020

Everything that was never released

The five tracks above are the entire public output. Behind them sits roughly 54 GB of recording sessions holding twenty-nine original songs and twenty-one covers that were written, tracked, and in most cases mixed — and then never put anywhere.

The titles are listed here because a title is the last part of a song to disappear. The recordings themselves are staying private.

The two metal arrangements — Reckless Love and Build My Life, both finished in early 2018 — are the strangest things in the folder and the most fun. Worship songs run through a drop-tuned guitar and a double kick. They are also the reason none of the covers are posted here: they are somebody else’s songs.

The last session

The final dated jam in the archive is April 10, 2020 — three weeks into the first lockdown. The last file anyone saved is a song called We Are Home, touched April 17, 2020. Nobody announced anything. There is a stray console test from February 2021 and then nothing.

Artwork

Both EPs got as far as finished cover art. Neither got as far as a track list. The Worship photograph is a long exposure of the dock and the treeline at night — the same picture the web site used, cropped into a starfield at the top of this page and a horizon at the bottom.

reachableheights.com, 2016 – 2018

The site

There were two of them. The first was a WordPress site running the “unicon” theme, live from May 2016, where all five songs were posted. Six posts total — five songs and one announcement. That announcement, from May 16, 2018, is the last thing the band ever published:

Revamping Our Online Presence

As we figure out what we want to accomplish in ministry and worship, we are continuing to figure out how to utilize our online presence. Expect more stuff coming here and all across the web soon!

The revamp did happen. WordPress came down and was replaced with a single static page — the logo, one SoundCloud player, and a copyright line reading Copyright © Reachable Heights 2016. The humans.txt file shipped with the template placeholders still in it, and js/main.js was zero bytes. The “more stuff coming soon” never arrived. The band kept recording for two more years and never posted again.

This page is built on the design of that last holding page: the same logo, the same night-sky background, the same treeline along the bottom.

About this archive

How this was put back together

This is an archive, built in 2026. It is not the band’s original site, and Reachable Heights is not active. The domain lapsed and was re-registered specifically to hold this page.

The words come from three places, and none of them were rewritten. The five song posts and the 2018 announcement were recovered verbatim from the WordPress RSS feed as captured by the Internet Archive on December 4, 2018. The logo, the night-sky photograph, and both EP covers are the original files, still in their layered source form. The catalog of unreleased songs was read off the recording sessions themselves.

The audio is not hosted here. Every player above is an embed pointing at the band’s own SoundCloud account, which is still online and still owned by the band. If it ever goes away, these players will go with it — that is the honest state of it.

Nothing was invented and nothing was corrected. Where a sentence reads oddly, that is how it was written in 2016.
